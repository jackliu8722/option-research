### Deribit 官方（已核实来源，2026-06）
本库标「已核实·Deribit 官方」的事实来自以下页面：
- [Fees – Deribit Support](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/25944746248989-Fees) — 期权手续费上限=期权价值 12.5%、清算费上限 25%
- [Settlement – Deribit Support](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/29734325712413-Settlement) — UTC 08:00 到期、交割价=到期前 30 分钟(07:30–08:00) 指数 TWAP、每 4 秒取样
- [Inverse Options – Deribit Support](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/31424939096093-Inverse-Options) — 反向期权乘数=1（1 BTC/ETH）、欧式现金交割
- [Inverse Perpetual – Deribit Support](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/31424954847133-Inverse-Perpetual) — BTC 反向永续面值 \$10/张
- [Funding Specifications – Deribit Support](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/31424939178397-Funding-Specifications) — 资金费率 8h 利率、毫秒连续计提、Premium Rate、阻尼带 ±0.025%、上限 ±0.5%
- [DVOL - Deribit Implied Volatility Index – Deribit Insights](https://insights.deribit.com/exchange-updates/dvol-deribit-implied-volatility-index/) — 30 天前瞻、方差互换法、两个到期、剔除 |delta|<5%
- [DVOL Futures – Deribit Support](https://support.deribit.com/hc/en-us/articles/31424954825373-DVOL-Futures) — DVOL 期货存在
